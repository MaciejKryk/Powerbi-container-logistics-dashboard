# Ocean Freight Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## Overview
This project presents an end-to-end **Ocean Freight Performance Dashboard** built in **Power BI**.  
The dashboard analyzes shipment performance across carriers, delay causes, and revenue trends to support operational and management-level decision making.

The main objective of this project is to identify **systemic drivers of delays**, rather than focusing on individual carrier performance.

---

## Key Business Questions
- What are the main causes of shipment delays?
- How does on-time performance vary across carriers?
- Are delays driven by specific carriers or by system-wide issues?
- How do performance trends impact revenue over time?

---

## Key Insights
- Shipment delays occur across **all carriers**, indicating a **system-wide operational issue** rather than isolated carrier underperformance.
- The most frequent delay causes are:
  - Customs Hold  
  - Documentation  
  - Weather  
  - Port Congestion
- On-time performance levels are relatively similar across carriers.
- Revenue trends remain stable despite fluctuations in delay causes, suggesting operational resilience.

---

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards:
  - Total Revenue
  - Total Profit
  - On-Time Performance (%)
  - Average Delay (days)
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Top causes of delays (dynamic ranking)
- On-time performance comparison by carrier
- Interactive slicers:
  - Region
  - Date range
  - Carrier
  - Delay reason
- Drill-through navigation
- Reset filters button for usability
